Reed switch-based liquid level sensor for agrochemical UAV tanks. Designed for harsh environments — no calibration needed, float-independent of liquid type.

## bullets
- Evaluated and proposed measurement principles: reed switch vs. capacitive vs. float-based approaches
- Designed and tested sensor configurations for agro-UAV operating conditions (vibration, liquid type variance)
- Contributed to sliding window median filter for robust signal in vibration-heavy environment
- Hardware: Reed Switches 4×28mm NC, STM32F103C8T6, UART output, 3.3–5V
- Part of Capstone 2025, federal program "Kadryi dlya BAS" (76 teams, 16 regions)
